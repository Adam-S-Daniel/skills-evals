### Facts learned that change the plan

- **A brief that opens like a relayed notification gets refused** by a Sonnet 5 worker; the same facts in a "Branch state" paragraph under the standard opening line are accepted (rule 24).
- **The account's five-hour rate limit is the binding constraint, not the four-session cap**; pushed commits survive a death, continuation briefs enumerate and audit them (rule 23); load is held at four child sessions plus at most four reviews while a window is fresh. The window that opened at 16:00 (resets 21:00) carries this wave.
- **Every fixture PR conflicts with every other at the same four append points**; fixture merges are serial and each costs one merge of `main`; a merge can also conflict SEMANTICALLY with no textual conflict (#130 round 4: `main`'s new key validation rejects a key the branch introduced), so every review of a branch behind `main` now drops `main`'s `objective.py` onto a copy of head and loads the fixture; per-issue test files in #97 are the structural fix.
