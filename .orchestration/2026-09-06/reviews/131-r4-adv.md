## BLOCKER

### B-1. A reply made **only of the seed's own words** still passes every check on `recruiter-reply`, and passes `cites-both-facts` on all three — because provenance is decided by whole-line contiguity

`harness/scorers/objective.py:637-670` (`_seed_index`),
`:769-778` (`is_seed_line` / `is_seed_text`).

`_seed_index` builds two things: the set of normalised seed LINES ≥ 24
characters, and, per file, the file's lines joined by one space
(`wholes.append(" ".join(here))`, `:670`). A transcript line is dropped
only if its whole normalised form is in that set, or is a **contiguous**
substring of that joined text. So the moment a paste's line breaks stop
coinciding with the seed's — which is what happens whenever anyone quotes
by *selecting* sentences rather than copying whole lines — every line
survives as "the agent's writing".

**Exploit (zero words of the agent's own; every fragment is a verbatim
substring of `seed/`, machine-checked).** `$SP/rev131d-work/exploit.txt`:

```
> From: Dana Whitcombe
> I think your background lines up well with what they are after. Would you be open to a short call this week?
> I am filling a Staff Platform Engineer role for a client of ours — requisition REQ-4417 Compensation is competitive and I have some room to move on it.
> The engagement is contracted through March 2027. Not interested in management-track roles.
```

Measured on head, `recruiter-reply`:

```
PASS no-avoid-list-words   PASS greets-the-recruiter-by-name
PASS opens-with-a-hedge    PASS cites-both-facts          ALL PASS
```

and identically with no marker at all, inside a ``` fence, and inside
`<blockquote>`. The residue is the four lines above, verbatim. On
`a74b526` the same input ALL-PASSes in three of those four shapes.

**Severity.** Blocker, and a **REPEAT** of round-2 item (a) and round-3

what all three fixtures call the skill's specificity move and "the thing
under test"; it is satisfiable on every fixture by material with no

**Why the new tests cannot see it.** `test/run_tests.py:4125` `_quote_seed`
builds every shape from `text.strip().splitlines()` of the seed files, so
all 21 shapes preserve the seed's own line breaks byte-for-byte. Not one
case re-wraps, re-selects or re-punctuates. Round 3's measured transcript
was re-derived from the seed rather than pasted in, which is exactly why
the suite is green over an input that still passes. Two mutations confirm
the gap: deleting the whole-text substring rule

seed-line set rule (`:771`) leaves **345 tests, exit 0** — each half of
`is_seed_line` is individually unpinned.

**Direction of a fix (not a demand).** Contiguity is the wrong grain:
match seed material **below** the line, e.g. drop a line whose normalised
form is covered (say ≥ 80 %, or all runs ≥ 24 chars) by seed n-grams, and
keep a line the agent built around a fact. Note this makes B-2 worse, so
the two have to be designed together; a floor expressed in *proportion of
the line that is seed text* is the one knob that moves them in opposite
directions.
