## The mechanism, measured

`_Relevance.fold` is `production.get(_base(x), _base(x))` — `_base` strips one
trailing `-DDDDDDDD` **unconditionally**. The map attribution actually uses,
`_usage_alias_map`, is built on `alias_map`, which creates the hop
`X-DDDDDDDD → X` **only when `X` is itself one of the ids it is given**. The two
relations are not the same, and the cap orders by the first while the shares are
computed through the second.

So an entry can be the ONLY id that keeps a census key's chain intact, while
`fold` says its whole group is already "covered" by some other entry — and
decision 4's tier 2 spends no slot on a covered group, which drops that entry to
tier 3, behind every `0filler-NNNN`. Probed through `_relevance`/`_usage_alias_map`
on head:
