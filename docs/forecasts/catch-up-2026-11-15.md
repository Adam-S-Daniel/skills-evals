## Levers on the re-work

- **Fewer rest changes.** Automation already merges agents-md changes only in the
  Monday window. The Nov 13 change was a thread's merge outside it, so the same
  week paid twice. Holding every agent merge of `agents-md/` to the window caps
  re-proofs at one a week. Merging only every other week halves them.
- **Let a final keep survive a rest change that leaves its section's text alone.**
  This would change Adam's 2026-11-07 rule, so it is his call. It removes the
  ~$107 a week.
- **Throttle new fixtures on undecided subjects.** Each new fixture adds two cells.
  A removal needs every cell, and the keep's confidence is split over more cells,
  so a new fixture on an undecided subject costs most where it helps least. Most
  requests come from the improve lane wanting its minimum of fixtures (the weekly
  sweep's `needs-fixtures`) and from hand-built harder-fixture sets. Proposal:
  1. Ask for `needs-fixtures` only once the subject's verdict is `keep`. Improving
     a subject that may yet be removed spends twice.
  2. Ask for `needs-harder-fixtures` on an undecided subject only when every one
     of its counted cells is parked at the ceiling, so no existing cell can move.
  3. Price each request as the batch fit prices a cell (`pairs_per_cell` times
     the model's pair cost, per roster model). File requests on undecided subjects
     only while their weekly total fits a fixed share of the cap.

## How the model works

- **Per cell:** the planner's own projection (`plan_week.pairs_to_verdict`) of the
  pairs before the keep or remove test decides, capped at the cell's remaining
  pairs. A cell with no measured gain yet costs `pairs_per_cell` from
  `batch-policy.yml`.
- **Per subject, deep-first:** a keep in any cell ends the subject; a removal
  needs every cell. Today's open subjects are 14 guidance keeps at ~$107 total, 2
  skill keeps (~$22), and 11 subjects heading for removal or the cap (~$429).
- **Cost:** each model's pair cost from the verdict document, scaled to the
  measured $0.53 a pair. The routine session adds $0.19 a fire, at 1.43 pairs a
  fire.
- **Capacity:** `budget.yml`'s weekly cap times $6.67 to $7.50 a point.
- **Keep-up:** each weekly arrival is priced at the median open subject ($9.89) or
  at the prior cells. Measured inputs and their sources are in
  [`assumptions-2026-11-15.yml`](assumptions-2026-11-15.yml); the full output is in
  [`forecast-2026-11-15.json`](forecast-2026-11-15.json).
