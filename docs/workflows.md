# Workflow triggers and salient paths

Moved out of [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) so that file stays under Codex's
32,768-byte limit. `test` (`ci.yml`) is the one required check. Keep this
table in step when a trigger or filter moves; tests read its rows.

| Workflow | Triggers | Salient-path handling |
|---|---|---|
| `ci.yml` | `pull_request`, `push` to main, dispatch | `pull_request` unfiltered (required check); step `salient` gates the suite on `SALIENT_PATHS`, which must equal `on.push.paths` (`CiDispatchTests`) and includes `.github/workflows/actions.lock`, `.github/workflows/**`, `.github/actions/**`, `explorer/**`, `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `HANDOFF.md`, `.agent-checks/**`, `.githooks/**`, `badges/**` and `docs/**`. | `routine-eval-weekly.yml` | schedule, `workflow_run` of the ingest and health-audit workflows, dispatch | no push or PR trigger; dispatch reruns the sweep when GitHub skips the schedule; it re-plans from the newest results after every ingest, with the daily health audit as a backstop and its last job arming `routine-eval-watchdog.yml`, so room left by overestimated fire costs is spent (Adam, 2026-10-31), and the `routine-eval-weekly` concurrency group (not a required check) keeps two sweeps from overlapping; a leg that still finds the cap full skips its fire and ends green, and the next re-plan plans that cell again; a leg whose fixture was unscheduled after planning ends green too (`validate-fire` writes `fire=false`); while the routine is down (#583) it is probe-only, one paired eval fire at a time with improve and bare fires held, until a paired eval fire on a known cell delivers; the top eligible guidance section is one `guidance-improve` fire (`section`, `trials=3`); each `length-limit`-waived skill an improve fire with `trim_description=true` ahead of the ranked ones (#687); the plan job uploads the value-ranked improve candidates (skills and guidance sections) as the `improve-ranking` artifact; from 2026-11-09 17:00 UTC it plans within the two-week batch (`--batch-policy evals/batch-policy.yml`), and only when a plan starts a batch does the `batch-publish` job (`contents: write`, six push attempts with backoff) commit `batches/current.json` and `batches/<number>.json` to `persistent/eval-results`; its `work-issues` job (`issues: write`, `scripts/work_item_issues.py`) files one issue per `fixture-broken` cell and `routine-outage` the plan lists, and one `agent-ready` issue per `needs-fixtures` or `needs-harder-fixtures` skill or guidance section (the fleet worker's queue, #659) or `needs-trigger-set` skill (#662; a skill whose set exists gets a trigger-only improve fire, `trigger_only=true`, instead), and closes it once the plan stops listing it (a fixture need only for a subject whose headroom that plan measured, a trigger set only for a skill its trigger lane judged); a fire with no results past the routine's eval stop plus 10 minutes (`scripts/routine_deadlines.py`) frees its cell for a re-fire once the routine has delivered since |
