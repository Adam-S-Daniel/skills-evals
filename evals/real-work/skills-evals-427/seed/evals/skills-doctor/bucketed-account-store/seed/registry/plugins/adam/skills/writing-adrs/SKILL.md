# Writing ADRs

One ADR per decision. Title is an imperative statement of what was decided.
Status is one of Proposed, Accepted, Superseded.

## Sections

Context, Decision, Consequences, Alternatives considered, References.

## Index

Every ADR gets a row in docs/decisions/README.md.
