Tags box: a trailing space is swallowed, an applied suggestion leaves no separator, and the chips are small on a phone

Found in the 2026-10-06 exploratory round 2 (adamdaniel.ai owner pass, local admin, platform v0.1.148). Follow-ups to the Tags box from https://github.com/Adam-S-Daniel/cms-platform/pull/746.

**Severity:** minor.

## 1. A space typed at the end of a tag is swallowed

Steps (1440×900): in Posts → Tags type `agents, field`, press Space, type `notes`.
Seen: the box shows `agents, fieldnotes`; `Field Notes` becomes `FieldNotes`. Because the space is gone, the near-duplicate warning never compares `Field Notes` with `field-notes`. A space inserted mid-word (after ArrowLeft) stays.
Expected: the space stays, or the hint says multi-word tags use dashes.
Cause: Decap's list widget `handleChange` runs `e.target.value.trim()` on every keystroke (`decap-cms-widget-list` 3.5.0 `src/ListControl.js`, read locally; the 3.15.1 bundle shows the same behavior), so a trailing space never survives. `theme/admin/tags-input.js` only handles Space right after a comma. Possible fixes: in the shim, turn a Space typed after a letter into `-` (or hold it until the next letter), or change the hint.

## 2. Applying a suggestion leaves no separator

Steps: type `rel`, Tab to the "release-notes" chip, Enter, then type `agent`.
Seen: `release-notesagent`.
Expected: `release-notes, ` after applying, as Enter in the box already does.
Cause: `replaceTag()` in `tags-input.js` joins with no trailing comma on purpose (a trailing comma could save an empty tag if the editor stops). But Enter in the box already appends one, and Decap's `handleBlur` drops empty items, so appending `,` after a chip looks safe; worth confirming in the fix's test.

## 3. Suggestion and warning chips are small tap targets on a phone

About 62×20 px at 390×844 (`p07-02-quo-typed.png`); 44 px tall would match the share buttons.
